Spreadsheet Cleanup Checklist

Use this checklist before sending a small business spreadsheet to a client, teammate, accountant, or buyer.

Request 10 USD cleanup Email spreadsheet brief

Basic cleanup pass

Column namesUse clear labels such as Customer Name, Email, Payment Status, Amount USD, and Notes.
Duplicate rowsCheck obvious duplicates by email, order ID, or another stable field.
Status valuesNormalize mixed values such as paid, PAID, complete, and done into one consistent label.
AmountsFormat money columns consistently and keep the currency visible.

Readable delivery pass

  • Freeze or highlight the header row if the file has many rows.
  • Remove blank rows that split the table into fragments.
  • Use short notes that explain the business meaning, not just abbreviations.
  • Keep original intent visible; do not invent customer, payment, or tax data.
  • Save the cleaned file with a clear name and date.

When a 10 USD cleanup fits

  • The file is small enough to review quickly.
  • The task is about visible formatting, structure, or obvious duplicate cleanup.
  • The expected result can be described in one or two sentences.
  • It is not accounting, tax advice, audit work, or data verification.
